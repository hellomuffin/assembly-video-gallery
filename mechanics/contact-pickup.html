What this test establishes

The task begins from a fresh scene and runs through approach, grasp, lift, turn, placement and release. The hands use finite joint limits and force limits. Hand-root controllers and an ideal workholding fixture remain simplified; this is not a full-human dynamics result.

Physical checks · Video provenance

How this motion was planned
  • Fit a grasp to the wooden member and check the entire finger-closing path for collisions.
  • Search approach and withdrawal routes against the current scene.
  • Execute joint and hand-root targets through force-limited controllers, while measuring contact pressure and slip.
  • Slow the two-hand turn according to the motion of both hands, so neither hand pulls the grasp apart.
  • Transfer the load to the fixture before opening and withdrawing the hands.

This is a programmed task sequence with geometric planning and feedback. It is not a learned human policy or a full-body dynamics controller.
